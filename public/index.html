Notices come in broken.
Nobody pays for a bad one.

A station notice is a screenshot, a forwarding, or four lines typed in Marathi at 2am. Meera reads the train, the station and the new time, and returns schema-valid JSON. When she cannot read it, the request fails with a 4xx and the verified payment is released — the caller keeps their money.
